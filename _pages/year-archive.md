Hi there! I'm **Gilian Ponte**, Assistant Professor of Marketing at the Rotterdam School of Management, Erasmus University Rotterdam.

## Research

My research focuses on **marketing analytics and privacy**, with a particular interest in **differential privacy, econometrics, and machine learning**. I study how firms and policymakers can protect individual privacy while still drawing meaningful insights from data, both for academic theory and practical decision-making.

My PhD dissertation received the **EMAC Doctoral Dissertation Award**. My work has also been featured in the

I am currently developing methods to quantify and manage privacy risks in experimental and modeling contexts, supported by grants from Google and other partners.

## Public Policy

Beyond academia, I actively contribute to privacy-related policymaking in the Netherlands. I have collaborated with agencies such as **UWV, DUO, TNO, CBS, and the Ministry of Justice and Security**. If you work in policy and are exploring data protection, feel free to get in touch.
